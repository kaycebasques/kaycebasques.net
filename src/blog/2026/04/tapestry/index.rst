=============
Warp and woof
=============

Today I learned some tapestry jargon:

* The warp is the vertical threads that are held on the loom.

* The woof a.k.a. weft is the horizontal threads that are inserted over and
  under the warp.

In a finished tapestry you usually can only see the woof. The warp is an
invisible foundation.
